Usage rules

the short version
  • One accent per view. Olive carries the action. If everything is green, nothing is.
  • Mono is for data only. Ticket IDs, IPs, model numbers, throughput. Never for prose.
  • Borders before shadows. Cards sit on a 1px line at rest; shadow appears on hover or overlay.
  • Dark bands are punctuation. Two or three per page maximum, never adjacent.
  • Motion is 200ms or nothing. No entrance animations on content the user came to read.
  • Never hard-code a hex. If a colour is not in this sheet, it does not go in the build.
