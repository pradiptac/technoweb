# Notifications, mentions and working together

*Who can use this: everybody who signs in to the console.*

The console tells you when something is waiting for you, lets you ask a
colleague about a record by name, and warns you when somebody else is editing
the same thing.

## The bell

The bell is at the top right of every console screen. On a phone it is in the
menu under your name, as **Notifications**.

A number on the bell is how many notifications you have not read yet. It is
checked about once a minute, so a new one can take up to a minute to appear.

Press the bell to see them. **Unread** shows what you have not opened; **All**
shows the last ninety days. Each line says what happened — for example
"Asha Rao assigned TW-2026-00041 to you." Press a line to go straight to that
ticket, lead, page or order; it is then marked read. **Mark all read** clears
the number.

You are told when:

- a colleague mentions you in a note;
- a ticket, lead or engineer visit is assigned to you;
- a meeting is booked with you as the host;
- a customer replies on a ticket assigned to you;
- an import or a backup you started has finished, failed, or is ready for you to
  review;
- one of your alerts goes off (chapter 35).

You are never told about something you did yourself, and never about a screen
your account cannot open.

## Mentioning a colleague

In these notes you can mention a colleague, and they are told in their bell:

- an **internal note** on a ticket (not a reply to the customer);
- a note on a **lead**;
- a note on a shop **order**;
- a **team note** on a page, post, product or any other record (below).

Type **@** and the first letters of their name. A list of people appears; pick
one with the mouse, or with the arrow keys and Enter. Their name is written into
the note.

Only people who can open that screen are offered. If you delete their name from
the note before you save it, they are not told. At most ten people are told
about one note.

## Team notes

Every page, blog post, knowledge article, case study, solution, service,
product, shop product, event, tender, vacancy, custom content entry, landing
page and section library item has **Team notes**, beside **History** at the top
of its edit screen. The number in brackets is how many notes it has.

Team notes are for the people who work on the record — "Can you check the hero
picture?", or why a price was changed. They are never shown on the website.
Press **Team notes**, write your note, mention anybody who should see it, and
press **Post note**. You can delete your own notes; an administrator can delete
any.

## Somebody else is editing

When a colleague has the same record open, a line at the top of the form says
so — "Ravi Menon has this open." — and, once they start changing it, "Ravi Menon
is also editing this — unsaved changes." If they save while you are still
working, it says "Ravi Menon saved a newer version at 14:32."

None of this stops you working. It tells you to talk to each other before you
both save.

## When your save is refused

If somebody saved the record after you opened it, your save is refused rather
than quietly replacing their work. A yellow box says who saved it and when.
Everything you typed is still in the form.

- **Load the newer version** reloads the screen with their changes. Your typing
  is kept as a draft: press **Restore** in the box that appears to bring it back,
  check it against what they did, and save again.
- **Save mine anyway** replaces their version with yours. Use it only when you
  are sure — their changes are not lost forever, because **History** keeps the
  version you replaced, and the activity log records that you overwrote it.

## Choosing how you are told

On **Your account**, in the **Notifications** card, **Mentions and assignments**
decides whether mentions and assignments also reach you by email and browser
notification (the same email and browser settings your alerts use). Switched
off, they still appear in the bell.

An administrator can turn these emails off for everybody in **Settings →
Notifications**.
