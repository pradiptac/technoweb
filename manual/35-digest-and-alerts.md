# Your digest and alerts

*Who can use this: Everybody chooses their own. Administrators set the defaults and can change anybody's.*

The console can tell you what needs you without your having to open it, in two
ways:

- **The digest** is a short email, daily or weekly, listing the figures that
  matter to your role and how each has changed since the last one.
- **Alerts** arrive within about five minutes of something needing you, by
  email and, if you want, as a notification in your browser.

Everything is on **Your account** (the menu under your name), in the
**Notifications** card.

## Choosing your digest

- **How often**: Off, Daily or Weekly. New staff start on the site's default,
  which is Weekly unless an administrator has changed it.
- **At**: the hour it arrives.
- **On**: the day of the week, for a weekly digest.
- **Skip a day when everything is zero**: switched on, a digest with nothing to
  report is not sent at all.

The digest uses the same figures as **Ask ALTIS** (chapter 30). A figure that is
zero is gathered into one "All clear" line at the end. A count that has changed
since your last digest shows how much it went up or down.

Press **Send me today's digest** to see one now. It does not count as your
scheduled digest.

## The alerts

You see only the alerts that belong to your role, and each one can be switched
off.

| Alert | Who gets it | When it is sent |
|---|---|---|
| A critical ticket nobody has taken | Support | after 30 minutes |
| A ticket past its due time | Support | as soon as it is overdue |
| A hot lead nobody has answered | Sales | after 2 hours |
| A paid order not yet sent | Store | after 2 days |
| A return nobody has answered | Store | after 2 days |
| Email not leaving | Administrators | after 10 minutes |
| A backup that failed or did not run | Administrators | as soon as it happens |
| The site health score fell | Administrators | by 10 points in a week |

The times are the defaults; an administrator can change them.

You are told **once** about each thing. If a ticket is taken and later becomes
critical and unassigned again, you are told again. Several new alerts at once
arrive together in one email.

An alert names the ticket, order, lead or return by its number. It never
contains a customer's name, address, telephone number or message.

## Getting alerts in this browser

1. Press **Get alerts in this browser** and allow notifications when the browser
   asks.
2. The browser appears in your **Browsers** list.
3. To stop, press **Remove** beside it.

If the button is missing and the card says "Browser notifications are not set up
on this site", the site's push service has not been connected. Chapter 12 says
how.

**Quiet hours** hold back browser notifications between the two times you choose
(21:00 to 08:00 to start with). Email still arrives during quiet hours. Press
**Send a test alert** to check that everything reaches you.

## For administrators

- **Settings → Notifications** sets:
  - the digest new staff start with;
  - which alerts the site sends at all;
  - how long each one waits.

  An alert switched off there is off for everybody, and shows as such on each
  person's card.
- **Staff → (a person)** shows that person's Notifications card. You can change
  their digest and alerts there. You cannot add a browser for them; each person
  does that on their own account.
