# The support network: service centres on a map

*Who can use this: Administrator.*

**Site → Support network** is where you list your service centres, offices and
service partners. Each one has an address, a telephone number and a pin on a
map. Your website shows them all on the **Support network** page
(`/support-network`), country by country and city by city, on a map you can
zoom into one country.

## Adding a centre

Press **New centre**. A centre has three tabs.

- **Details**: the name visitors know it by, its telephone numbers, email and
  opening hours, and what kind of place it is (service centre, office or
  service partner). **Shown on the website** takes it off the page without
  deleting it.
- **Location**: the country, state, city and address. The list on the website
  is grouped by country and then by city. To put the centre on the map, do
  one of these:
  - press the map where the centre is;
  - type its latitude and longitude;
  - paste a Google Maps link and press **Use link**. The coordinates are read
    from the link on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

  A centre without a pin is still listed, just not drawn on the map.
- **Staff**: the people who work there. Add a staff member, give them a title
  such as "Field engineer", and choose whether their name is **on the
  website**. Only the name and title are ever shown — never a staff member's
  email or mobile.

Press **Save centre**. The website shows the change at once.

## Staff and centres

A staff member can be tagged to more than one centre. On **System → Staff**
each person's centres are listed, and the number in a centre's Staff column
opens the staff list filtered to that centre.

## The map

The map is drawn by your website itself: there are no map tiles from another
company, nothing is loaded from Google, and no cookie is set. India's
boundary is drawn as India officially shows it. Each centre's card has a
**Directions** link that opens Google Maps only when somebody presses it.

When all your centres are in one country, the map opens zoomed to that
country. With centres in several countries, visitors can choose a country
above the map.

## On other pages

The **Support network** section in the page builder puts the same map and
centres on any page. It can open on one country. Add the page to your menus
with the **Support network** item; it hides itself while no centre is shown.

## Things to know

- Deleting a centre does not change the staff accounts tagged to it.
- A demo install comes with three sample centres named "Sample …". Delete
  them before you go live.
